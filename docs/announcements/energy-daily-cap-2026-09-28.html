📣 A note from the merrymen team — not from your agent. We're borrowing its chat once to tell you about a change to how hosted agents work; it goes back to speaking for itself straight after. Your agent now runs on energy, and there is a daily cap. A hosted agent runs at full strength while your wallet and the agent's own account hold 100,000 $MERRYMEN between them. Below that it keeps running, on about a tenth of a standard day: a paced share of its AI reviews and a small number of new trades it opens on its own (2 a day at the default limits). The allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. What does not change: stop-losses, take-profits and the orders you place yourself always run. Your signed limits, your funds and your keys are exactly as they were. When today's allowance is used up, your agent tells you once, with its account address and how much is missing. Two ways to full strength: 1 · Send $MERRYMEN on Robinhood Chain to your agent's account (the address on the funding panel), or keep it in a wallet you have linked in Settings → Merry Circle. Both count. 2 · Send USDG to your agent's account and ask it in the Merrymen app chat (not here) to get its $MERRYMEN. You confirm the most it may spend; it buys what is missing with a small margin, inside the limits you signed. Its key can buy $MERRYMEN but can never sell or send it. If your key is older than this change, re-sign once at /grant — free, and nothing moves. Agents in practice mode won't spend real USDG on it; send $MERRYMEN instead. Or change nothing, and your agent carries on at the reduced pace. $MERRYMEN is utility only: it buys capacity, nothing else, and we make no promise about its price. Questions go to the beta group, not to this chat. 🏹
